Who we are

From the city. For the people.

Bird Gang Studio is a Philly design studio for everyday clothing and accessories. We take what's already in your closet, like the game-day jacket or the hoodie that's been through three playoff runs, and make it new again.

When nothing out there fits the idea, we make it from scratch. Screen printed, sewn and embroidered right here in the city.

Our mission

Create, design and deliver one-of-a-kind lifestyle goods that are affordable and a Philly thang.
